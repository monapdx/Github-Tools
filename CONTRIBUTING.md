# CONTRIBUTING GUIDELINES

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **[Github-Tools](https://monapdx.github.io/Github-Tools/)**.

For full transparency, I am an independent creator and enthusiast who does not have a team of others to help with tasks like moderation. I do my best to respond to submitted issues, pull requests and other forms of contact in a timely manner. If reaching me through Github is proving slower than you'd like, you're always also welcome to email me directly at **ashly@purelymail.com**.

## WAYS TO CONTRIBUTE

I'm open to most any kind of collaboration you may be interested in. I encourage others to reach out, suggest improvements, request features, provide user feedback, cross-promote related tools and projects, or even work together on any new concept that arises from exposure to my existing efforts.

If you **fork** any of my Github repos and make an improvement to any of my tools, I would love you to **submit a pull request** with good documentation of the changes made and what they accomplish. If your changes enhance my tools, I am more than willing to merge your pull requests!

Of course, we don't have to be in touch directly for you to make a meaningful contribution.

In fact, the two types of help I happen to be in the most need of at this point in time are:

1. **Distribution and amplification:** if you find my tools useful and think others might benefit from them, I strongly urge you to share them with your friends, colleagues or clients. This is one of the absolute most powerful things you can do help me out right now. I do not use social media sites and I do not have a professional network to draw from either. So if you do, please share links to my repos with your people!
2. **Modest sponsorship:** if any of my tools helped you and you are in a position to make a small donation or a small monthly contribution, that would be enormously helpful as well. The easiest option would be to become a **[Sponsor](https://github.com/sponsors/monapdx)** right here on Github!
